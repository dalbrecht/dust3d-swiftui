# ADR 0037: Aspect-aware mesh camera fitting

Status: Accepted.

Issue: #73.

The renderer normalizes mesh bounds to a unit-sphere envelope. A fixed camera
at distance three fits a square viewport but clips separated parts in portrait
previews: the horizontal field of view shrinks with aspect ratio. Fit previously
restored the same clipped projection.

Scale viewing distance by the inverse of the smaller of aspect and one. Keep
stored distance, yaw and pitch unchanged so resizing preserves relative zoom and
orbit, and retain landscape behavior. Grow the far plane with viewing distance
so extreme portrait sizes do not move the model beyond the depth range. Clamp
unavailable finite aspect ratios to 0.01 and use square projection for nonfinite
ratios.

Test projection independently from Metal using sampled sphere points, bounded
zoom/orbit and a real two-part mesh generated by the pinned core. Enforce 100%
line coverage for this pure camera only. Native rendered verification remains
necessary for draw submission, materials and interaction; no GPU coverage is
inferred from projection tests. No external dependency or core revision changes.
